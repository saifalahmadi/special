## How to Read Status

- **VERIFIED** — citation exists at the stated reporter/docket, holding matches the claimed proposition, any quotes confirmed verbatim against source text
- **UNVERIFIED — DROPPED** — citation could not be confirmed against primary authority; removed from draft
- **UNVERIFIED — FLAGGED** — citation could not be confirmed; retained in draft with explicit "UNVERIFIED" label per user instruction (not recommended)
